# 🥬 Kijani Kilo — Online Grocery Store

**Fresh Choices. Fair Portions. Convenient Shopping.**

Kijani Kilo is an online grocery shopping platform designed to make purchasing fresh produce and everyday groceries simple, convenient, and accessible. Customers can explore a variety of grocery products, compare prices by weight, add items to their shopping cart, and prepare their orders through an easy-to-use digital shopping experience.

The platform focuses on fresh produce, intuitive navigation, responsive design, and a smooth shopping experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 🌿 Key Features

- **Product Catalogue:** Browse a variety of fresh fruits, leafy greens, vegetables, root vegetables, and other grocery essentials.
- **Product Categories:** Explore products organized into clear categories for easier navigation.
- **Shopping Cart:** Add products, adjust quantities, remove items, and review selected groceries.
- **Weight-Based Pricing:** Display product prices per kilogram where applicable.
- **Product Search:** Find specific groceries quickly.
- **Product Filtering:** Filter products by category and other supported criteria.
- **Cart Summary:** View selected items, quantities, and the calculated order total.
- **Responsive Design:** Enjoy a user-friendly experience across different screen sizes.
- **Intuitive Navigation:** Move easily between the homepage, shop, product categories, and cart.
- **Favicon and Branding:** Maintain a consistent visual identity throughout the website.

## 🛒 How It Works

1. Visit the Kijani Kilo website.
2. Browse the available grocery products.
3. Search for specific items or filter products by category.
4. Select products and specify the required quantities.
5. Add the selected items to the shopping cart.
6. Review the cart and confirm the order details.
7. Proceed through the checkout or order-submission process, depending on the features enabled on the website.

## 🔒 Security and Data Handling

Security and customer privacy should be considered throughout development.

- Validate user input before processing orders.
- Avoid storing sensitive payment information in browser storage.
- Use HTTPS when the website is deployed.
- Protect customer information and restrict access to administrative functionality.
- Integrate a secure backend and payment provider if online payments are supported.

**Important:** A frontend built only with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript does not independently provide secure server-side order processing, authentication, or payment handling.
